State Expansion Rankings

Ranks every state outside Massachusetts and New Jersey (Doorman's existing markets) on five factors: Compatibility (30%), Decision-Window Timing (25%), Market Concentration (20%), Legislative Direction (15%), and Market Size / TAM (10%). A state classified Restricted on the main tracker is excluded outright, regardless of score — see How It Works for the full methodology.
