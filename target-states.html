Methodology

This list re-ranks itself automatically every time the tracker updates — it's computed straight from the same compatibility, legislation, and funding data on the other two pages, not maintained by hand. Massachusetts and New Jersey are excluded as existing Doorman markets, and any state with a hard physical-storage mandate (Restricted) is excluded entirely as incompatible. Everything else is scored:

Permitted+5State law explicitly allows a software/technology approach
Ambiguous+2Restriction law doesn't name a method, so software isn't ruled out
No Law+1Fully open to district-level adoption, no statewide rule either way
Active legislation+3A bill in progress creates a near-term compliance deadline — when districts actually shop for a solution
Funded+1State has earmarked money for compliance tools, shortening the purchase cycle
Manually verified+1Classification has been checked against a primary source, lowering the risk it flips later
